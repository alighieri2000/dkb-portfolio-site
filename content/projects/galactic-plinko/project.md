A custom, sci-fi-themed housing that turns an off-the-shelf PLINKO board into a branded Galactic Couture installation, designed, modeled, and fabricated to pressure-fit together with minimal on-site setup. Built for the client's booth at the Star Trek Las Vegas convention.

## What I made

- Modeled a modular frame and enclosure around the stock board.
- Engineered it to pressure-fit assemble, so setup at the event is fast and needs almost no tools.
- 3D-printed the parts, finished them in metallic paint, and assembled the unit with a clear front and LED-lit accents.
- Delivered the complete build in a five-day turnaround.
