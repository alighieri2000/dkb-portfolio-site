Life-sized models inspired by LEGO knights, built for a school-thesis spec commercial. I handled the full digital-to-physical pipeline: modeling and retopology, print optimization, printing, sanding, assembly, and painting.

## Pipeline

- Modeled and retopologized the pieces for clean, watertight, printable geometry.
- Optimized for FDM printing: orientation, sectioning, tolerances, supports.
- Printed life-sized components, including child-sized knight armor and a life-sized plume.
- Finished by hand: sanding, seam fill, priming, and airbrushed paint for a LEGO-accurate look.
