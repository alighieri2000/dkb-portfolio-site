Design and Process

This piece is a digital sculpture of a minimum wage worker set in a cyberpunk world, alongside her robotic micro-manager. She has a company-provided cybernetic back brace to ensure constant productivity.

Sculpted in Zbrush, textured using Substance Painter, rendered using Marmoset Toolbag
