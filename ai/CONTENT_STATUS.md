Facts Dante gave that the pages rely on:

- Remmick: the villain of the film Sinners; scratch-made wearable effects with a fog effect built into the costume; bespoke-fit accessories via 3D scanning, sculpting and printing. Year 2025.
- Bloodhound: Whistling Winter skin from Apex Legends, modeled from leaked footage before the skin shipped; FDM prints, EVA foam, sewn garments, micro LEDs.
- Cashout Station: life-size prop from The Finals, designed to come apart into one-person pieces, as light and cheap as possible without reading as a lightweight prop.
- Six Bullets: Guido Mista's Stand from JoJo's Bizarre Adventure as durable, posable convention props with a custom pose rig.
- ARC Wasp: articulated 3D-printed Wasp from ARC Raiders with an illustrated assembly guide Dante wrote.
- LEGO Knight: life-sized armor and props for a student thesis spec commercial; fan work, not affiliated with the LEGO Group (keep that line).
- Galactic PLINKO: sci-fi housing for a stock PLINKO board for Galactic Couture's booth at Star Trek Las Vegas; pressure-fit assembly; five days from spec to delivery.
- Emma Frost (now titled Self-Illuminating Diamonds): vacuum-formed PETG diamonds from a home-built vacuum former, dichroic film cut on a Cricut, 1 mm EVA foam form under stretch fabric. Dante: "Emma is the diamonds."
